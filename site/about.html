About Ansar

Frontline safety leadership, accredited training and AI — in one professional.

Ansar builds HSE systems that protect people and pass audits — and trains the teams that run them. His career spans construction, oil & gas, infrastructure and corporate HSE across the Gulf and beyond.

What sets him apart is a rare blend of chartered safety leadership, accredited training, and AI & data science. Clients don't just get compliance — they get foresight: dashboards, predictive insight and digital tools that turn safety from a cost into a competitive advantage.

Beyond consultancy, Ansar mentors professionals worldwide toward CSP, ASP, CRSP, PMP and other international certifications, drawing on the credential stack he holds himself. His philosophy is simple: practical, evidence-led, and people-first.
